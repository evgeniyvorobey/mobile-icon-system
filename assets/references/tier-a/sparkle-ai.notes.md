## Why this tier
Three paths: large 4-point sparkle + small `+` + tiny dot. **Big sparkle has 1, 2, 2, 1-type tip-radii** (very sharp tips, soft inside corners) — opposite of star's pattern. Two satellite accents (a `+` and small filled dot) sit in opposite corners, breaking symmetry intentionally so sparkle reads as "magic/AI" rather than "star/ranking".

## What a generator should learn
AI sparkle ≠ star. Star is symmetric and centered; sparkle has off-center accents and asymmetric weight.
