## Why this tier
Two overlapping square message bubbles with offset tails. Multi-thread / conversation-with-multiple-people metaphor. Front bubble overlaps and partly obscures back bubble — back bubble's right edge is omitted exactly where front would overlap, creating clean depth without `z-index`.

## What a generator should learn
Multi-message = two overlapping square bubbles with depth created by occlusion-by-omission, not stacking.
