## Why this tier
Two overlapping rounded rectangles offset by (4, 4)pt. **Both rectangles' top-left corners must visibly overlap** — if front rectangle covers back's top-left corner, icon reads as "single rectangle with shadow" instead of "two rectangles".

## What a generator should learn
Copy ≠ clipboard; copy = two overlapping rectangles with both top-left corners visible.
