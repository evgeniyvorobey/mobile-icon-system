## Why this tier
16pt-mini exemplar of the X / close metaphor. Two stroked diagonals at 24pt become one continuous filled glyph at 16pt. Crossing point is rendered as a filled lozenge (not two intersecting lines) so the cross stays solid at native size. Bar widths are proportionally chunkier than 24pt strokes because 16pt has no room for half-stroke anti-aliasing.

## What a generator should learn
16pt X = single filled glyph with thick bars and a solid lozenge at the crossing; never two intersecting strokes.
