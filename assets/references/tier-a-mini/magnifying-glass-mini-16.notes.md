## Why this tier
16pt-mini exemplar of the search metaphor. 24pt outline = circle + line at 45°; 16pt-mini = single fill with a circular cutout for the lens (silhouette of glass + handle as one shape) so the lens still reads as transparent without needing a stroked ring. Handle thickened proportionally to survive 16pt — at native size a 1-unit handle would be one pixel after anti-aliasing.

## What a generator should learn
16pt search = single fill with cutout for the lens; handle thickened to ~3 native units to survive 16pt rasterization.
