## Why this tier
16pt-mini exemplar of the check metaphor. The 24pt check is a stroke; the 16pt-mini check converts that stroke to a closed wedge fill. Wedge widens at the joint (where the up-stroke meets the down-stroke) so the tick reads with weight at native size. Vertex angle tighter than the 24pt version because the mini canvas can't accommodate a soft turn.

## What a generator should learn
16pt check is a closed wedge fill, not a stroked path; vertex tighter and joint thicker than 24pt outlined check.
