## Why this tier
Four-point sparkle silhouette with no satellites.

## What's missing vs tier-A
Single 4-point shape with no satellite accents — reads more like a star. Demonstrates difference between sparkle-as-decoration (Phosphor) and sparkle-as-AI-affordance (Lucide with accents).

## What a generator should learn
AI sparkle needs off-center satellite accents (`+` and dot) to differentiate from a generic 4-point star.
