## Why this tier
The lobes are perfectly symmetric (`-4.312` mirrored to `-4.313`, off by one rounding unit). No optical asymmetry.

## What's missing vs tier-A
Phosphor and Lucide hearts deliberately tilt right by ~5%. Heroicons' is too geometrically pure to feel emotional.

## What a generator should learn
Heart icons need intentional left/right asymmetry — perfect symmetry reads as a clinical glyph, not a liked-content marker.
