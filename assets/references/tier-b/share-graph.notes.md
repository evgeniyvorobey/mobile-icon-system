## Why this tier
Three-circle "node graph" metaphor. Reads as "share to social network" but is harder to recognize at 20pt than the box-with-arrow tray metaphor.

## What's missing vs tier-A
A more universal metaphor; this version requires an absolute size to read.

## What a generator should learn
Choose metaphors that survive size reduction. Three-circle node graphs flatten into a confusing triangle of dots at 16-20pt; the box+arrow tray remains readable.
