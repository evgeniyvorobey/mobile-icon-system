## Why this tier
Clock with hands at 3:00 — minute hand at 12, hour hand at 3.

## What's missing vs tier-A
Reads 3:00 (hour at 3, minute at 12) — flat horizontal hand combined with vertical, no smile. Demonstrates that 10:10 is deliberate convention; 3:00 is geometric default.

## What a generator should learn
Choose 10:10 over 3:00 — 10:10 is industry convention; 3:00 looks like a default an engineer drew.
