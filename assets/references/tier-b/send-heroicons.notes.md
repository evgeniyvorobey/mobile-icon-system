## Why this tier
Paper-airplane silhouette with curved wing edges.

## What's missing vs tier-A
Uses arc-curves on wing edges, making wings look "puffy". Paper airplanes have straight wings; curve is decorative.

## What a generator should learn
Paper airplane needs straight wings — curved wings break the metaphor (real folded paper has flat planes).
