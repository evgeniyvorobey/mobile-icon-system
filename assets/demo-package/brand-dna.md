# Brand DNA - Tidepool Tasks

Project: Tidepool Tasks
Mode: compact generated demo package
Brand DNA source: fictional product brief, filesystem-only
Accessibility tier: WCAG AA

## Product Character

Tidepool Tasks helps people collect the day, sort it into a simple plan, and trust that nothing important drifts away. The icon system should feel quiet, deliberate, and approachable without becoming playful decoration.

## Visual DNA

| Dimension | Decision | Icon effect |
|---|---|---|
| Geometric alphabet | Rounded rectangles, shallow arcs, compact circles | Icons share soft containers and repeated dot/check details. |
| Stroke language | 1.75pt round stroke with short orthogonal runs | Small sizes keep enough weight at 16pt and 20pt. |
| Terminal style | Round caps and joins | Ends feel gentle and match the app's calm task tone. |
| Corner treatment | 2.5pt outer radius, 1.5pt inner radius | Tab glyphs feel related without becoming identical. |
| Color logic | Monochrome masters, platform tinted | State must survive single-color and forced-color rendering. |
| Optical correction | Directional marks sit 0.25pt above geometric center when needed | Checks and route lines avoid appearing low in the tab bar. |

## Constraints

- Use one tintable shape language across iOS Tab Bar, Android Bottom Nav, and action buttons.
- Do not rely on color alone to distinguish selected and unselected states.
- Avoid literal water imagery; "Tidepool" is a brand tone, not the icon metaphor.
