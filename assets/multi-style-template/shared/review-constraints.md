## Apples-to-Apples Locks

## Allowed Variation

- Stroke or fill language.
- Terminal and corner treatment.
- Color model.
- Detail density.
- Optical correction strength.
- Overall style character.

## Not Allowed

- Changing icon count in only one style.
- Replacing a metaphor in only one style.
- Changing grid, accessibility tier, or target surfaces per style.
- Borrowing parts from another style before the winner is locked.
